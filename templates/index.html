Common SQL Patterns for Health Informatics

-- Patient lookup by MRN
SELECT * FROM patients WHERE mrn = 'MRN-000001';

-- Active encounters with department
SELECT e.*, d.name AS department
FROM encounters e
JOIN departments d ON e.department_id = d.id
WHERE e.status = 'active';

-- Lab results for a patient (with abnormal flagging)
SELECT lr.test_name, lr.value, lr.unit,
       lr.reference_range_low, lr.reference_range_high,
       lr.abnormal_flag
FROM lab_results lr
JOIN patients p ON lr.patient_id = p.id
WHERE p.mrn = 'MRN-000001'
ORDER BY lr.result_date DESC;

-- Medication orders with drug names
SELECT mo.*, m.name AS medication
FROM medication_orders mo
JOIN medications m ON mo.medication_id = m.id
WHERE mo.patient_id = 1;

-- Department census (active patients by department)
SELECT d.name, COUNT(e.id) AS active_patients
FROM departments d
LEFT JOIN encounters e ON d.id = e.department_id
  AND e.status = 'active'
GROUP BY d.id ORDER BY active_patients DESC;

-- Diagnosis frequency
SELECT dx.icd10_code, dx.description, COUNT(*) AS freq
FROM diagnoses dx
GROUP BY dx.icd10_code
ORDER BY freq DESC LIMIT 20;

HIPAA Quick Reference

Protected Health Information (PHI) includes: names, dates, phone/fax numbers, email addresses, SSN, MRN, account numbers, biometric identifiers, photos, and any other unique identifying number.

Minimum Necessary Standard: Access only the minimum PHI needed to perform your job function.

Break the Glass: Emergency override access that is audited and must be justified.

Audit Trail: All access to patient records must be logged with user ID, timestamp, action performed, and patient identifier.
